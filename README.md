One original line (120 characters), in the voice of a hardware wallet refusing to sign at 3 AM:

> At 3 AM I don't take orders from a sleep-deprived stranger who happens to know my PIN — come back when you're you again.

The angle is identity, not hardware: at 3 AM the person holding the PIN is not the person who set it — a sleep-deprived stranger wearing the owner's face. The wallet is a bouncer with a grudge, not a helpdesk: it refuses the signature and sets its own condition for coming back. No battery, no firmware update, no cold storage — the hour is the joke, and the line could not be pasted onto a 3 PM transaction.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
